Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 2026

Welcome to use the "Calendar Alarm" App. We attach great importance to your personal privacy and data security. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use, store and protect your data. By downloading, installing and continuing to use this App, you acknowledge that you have read, understood and agreed to all terms of this Privacy Policy.

1. Information Collection and Usage

1. All data such as schedules, alarms, reminders, repeat rules and notes of this App are only stored locally on your mobile phone, and will not be uploaded to the cloud or developer servers.

2. This App does not require mandatory account registration, and does not collect sensitive personal information such as mobile phone number, name or ID number.

3. To realize holiday scheduling and intelligent judgment of working days/rest days, the App will access public statutory holiday interface data through the network, which is only cached locally and will not be associated with any personal privacy information.

2. Network Usage and Third-Party Services

1. This App only calls the public third-party holiday API to obtain national statutory holidays and leave adjustment arrangements. It only acquires public holiday data and will not upload any personal schedules or private data.

2. Network access is only used for holiday data updates. There is no background data transmission for advertisements or unauthorized privacy reporting.

3. Except for the holiday interface, this App does not integrate any third-party statistics, advertising or tracking SDKs.

3. Device Permission Description

The permissions requested by the App are only to ensure on-time alarm reminders, background operation and reliable ringing, and will never be used for privacy theft or background information collection:

Notification permission: For pop-up reminders and alarm notifications;
Exact alarm permission: Ensure on-time ringing and scheduled reminders;
Boot startup: Restore alarm and reminder services after device restart;
Foreground service / Wake lock: Prevent the system from killing background processes and avoid alarm failure;
Vibration permission: Vibration feedback for alarm reminders;
Ignore battery optimization: Prevent the system power saving policy from closing alarm services.

You may turn off permissions in system settings at any time, which may cause abnormal functions such as alarm silence and reminder failure.

4. Data Storage and Security

1. All schedules, alarms and reminder data are stored only in the private local database of your device, accessible only by this App.

2. We will not share, sell or lend your schedule and alarm content to any third parties actively.

3. After uninstalling the App, all local data will be deleted together with the application. If system cloud backup is enabled, it shall be the responsibility of the mobile system, and this App will not participate in backup.

5. Minor Use

1. This App is suitable for users of all ages.

2. Minors shall use the App under the guidance and supervision of parents or guardians.

3. We will not intentionally collect any sensitive personal information from minors.

6. Privacy Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy timely according to function iteration and compliance requirements. The updated policy will be displayed inside the App. Your continued use of the App will be deemed acceptance of the revised Privacy Policy.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, function usage or privacy security, you may contact us through app store channels or developer message channels.
